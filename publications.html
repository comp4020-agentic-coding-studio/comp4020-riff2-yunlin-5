The journal, the software, the meeting

TUGboat, TeX Live, and the annual conference

TUG's public-facing work comes in three parts: a journal, a software distribution, and a conference. None of them require membership to use, but membership is what pays for them.

TUGboat, the journal

Three issues a year since 1980

TUGboat: The Communications of the TeX Users Group has run since the group's founding and now publishes three issues a year: tutorials, technical articles, conference proceedings, and community news. Members get the current issue as it is published, in print and, for the technical articles, online. Older issues become freely available to everyone after a delay.

Anyone can propose an article. There is a public style guide, a submission deadline for each issue, and an editorial ethics statement covering how submissions are handled.

TeX Live, the software

Free, and released every year

TeX Live is the free, cross-platform distribution most people actually install to get a working TeX and LaTeX system: binaries and packages for Linux, Windows, and, as MacTeX, macOS, maintained jointly by TUG and TeX user groups worldwide. It is free regardless of membership, most major Linux distributions package it directly, and it ships a new release each year.

It travels with the rest of the TeX Collection, which bundles the distributions with a snapshot of CTAN.

The annual conference

TUG 2026 was Calgary, TUG 2027 is Paris

TUG holds an annual conference, often jointly with a regional user group such as EuroTeX, bringing people together across the full range of experience, from a first LaTeX document to font engineering. Talks and proceedings are published afterwards through TUGboat, and recordings go online.

Announcements go out on the public tex-meetings mailing list, which anyone can join or browse without being a member, and TUG welcomes proposals from groups anywhere in the world willing to host a future meeting.

Books, interviews, and the store

The rest of what TUG publishes or sells, most of it feeding straight back into the group's funding.

Bookstore and reviews

Reviews of TeX and typography books, published in TUGboat and collected online, alongside member discounts from several publishers. The latest is LaTeX Beginner's Guide, third edition.

Interview corner

Long-form interviews with the people who write the software, design the fonts, and edit the journals, republished in TUGboat.

TUG store

Back issues, media, and merchandise, including the Lucida font family, whose sales support the group directly.

Other groups' journals

DANTE's Die TeXnische Komodie, GUST's Biuletyn, and the rest of the publications from TeX groups worldwide.
