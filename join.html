Calendar-year memberships

Join or support TUG

TUG has no other source of income. Membership dues and donations are what fund CTAN, the archive nearly every TeX installation pulls from, the TeX Live distribution, the TUGboat journal, and the annual conference. Joining is the straightforward way to keep those going.

Ways to join

Dues cover the calendar year regardless of when in the year you join, and all of it can be paid online. If you would rather pay by cheque or money order, TUG still takes paper mail.

New member

A lighter trial membership, with full benefits, before committing to the full rate. One year only.

Trial membership

Individual member

The full membership, renewed each January. Automatic renewal is available, and an early-bird rate runs at the start of the year.

Join or renew

Institution or patron

Universities, publishers, and companies join at a separate rate that covers several individual memberships.

Institutional membership

Joining a sister group as well? A joint-membership discount applies with DANTE (Germany), GuIT (Italy), GUTenberg (France), and NTG (the Netherlands). See TeX user groups worldwide.

What membership includes

  • A year of TUGboat

    Online access to the current issue's technical articles, and each issue delivered in print.

  • TUG-distributed software and fonts

    Including physical media where it is still offered, though TeX Live itself has always been free to download regardless of membership.

  • Member rates at TUG events

    Reduced registration for the annual conference and other meetings the group runs.

  • A vote in the group

    Members elect the board, and the elections are public.

What it is for

TUG's aims, unchanged since its bylaws were written, come down to three things: keep TeX, LaTeX, and Metafont in wide, reliable use; keep them portable and intact as software and operating systems change around them; and keep funding the people pushing high-quality electronic typesetting forward. Dues are what make the first two possible and the third worth attempting.

Not ready to join, or already a member of a sister group elsewhere? A direct donation is equally welcome and, for US taxpayers, generally tax-deductible. Donations can be directed at specific work, such as the TeX development fund.
