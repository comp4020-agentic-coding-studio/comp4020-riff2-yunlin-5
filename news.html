Latest news

  1. Journal

    TUGboat 47:1 is available

    Online and from the TUG store, and mailed to current members. Prior issue 46:3 is now publicly available. The next issue will be the TUG 2026 proceedings, with a papers deadline of 26 July 2026; submissions for the next regular issue are due 23 October 2026.

  2. Software

    TeX Live 2026 and MacTeX 2026 have been released

    Distributed primarily online through CTAN. More information on the TeX Live and MacTeX package pages.

  3. Books

    New book review: LaTeX Beginner's Guide, third edition

    By Stefan Kottwitz, reviewed by Uwe Ziegenhagen. TUG keeps a running set of reviews and discounted titles in its bookstore.

  4. Publicity

    A new TUG membership poster

    Oriented towards STEM departments and organisations. Posts of the one-page flyer, electronic or physical, are much appreciated. Other material lives in stickers and publicity.

Recent events

  1. July 2026

    TUG 2026

    Alt Hotel Calgary East Village, Calgary, Canada, 17 to 19 July, with a LaTeX developers' workshop the day before. Videos for all talks are available. TUG 2027 will be held in Paris in the summer.

  2. July 2026

    OSSConf 2026

    University of Zilina, Slovakia, 1 to 3 July, with a dedicated TeX and R session and several TeX-related workshops.

  3. June 2026

    Accessible Mathematical Content in PDF

    A webinar presented on 16 June by LaTeX team members and colleagues, available from the PDF Association along with related resources.

  4. April 2026

    EuroBachoTeX 2026

    Bachotek, Poland, 29 April to 3 May, on the theme TeX vs. AI. The call for papers and other details are on the GUST site.

  5. March 2026

    Frank Mittelbach receives an honorary doctorate

    Masaryk University Brno recognised his life's work on document engineering and on making LaTeX what it is today, with a public ceremony and talk on 19 March.
